# 369 – Sports Team Management

**Date:** 2026-05-02

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Coaches, team administrators, and sports organisations at every level — from youth clubs to semi-professional leagues — struggle to manage the operational complexity of running a team. Scheduling, roster management, player performance tracking, communication, and scouting are handled across disconnected apps, spreadsheets, and messaging threads. Elite clubs have access to expensive enterprise platforms, but the vast mid-market of amateur, youth, and semi-pro organisations lacks affordable, integrated tooling that combines administrative workflow with meaningful performance analytics and scouting capabilities.

By 2026, dynamic scheduling engines that automatically resolve multi-team and multi-venue conflicts have emerged as a key differentiator, eliminating reliance on external spreadsheets.

---

## 3. Key Features to Build

- **Roster management** – player profiles with contact details, position, jersey number, eligibility status, and document storage (contracts, medical clearances)
- **Scheduling engine** – season schedule builder with conflict detection across venues, officials, and opponent availability; automated reminders
- **Performance analytics** – session and match statistics per player; trend analysis; comparison against positional benchmarks
- **Scouting module** – prospect database with video tagging, attribute ratings, and comparison reports for recruitment decisions
- **Team communication hub** – announcements, direct messaging, and RSVP confirmation with delivery tracking
- **Training plan builder** – session design with drill libraries, load monitoring, and attendance logging
- **Injury and availability tracker** – player availability flags, return-to-play protocols, and medical staff notes
- **Financial management** – membership fee collection, kit orders, venue hire invoicing, and expense tracking per team

---

## 4. Technical Considerations

- Multi-sport configurability: sport-specific stat templates (goals, assists, tackles for soccer; points, rebounds, assists for basketball; etc.)
- Video integration: clip upload, tagging, and playlist assembly for coaching review and scouting highlight reels
- API integration with common sports data providers (Opta, Wyscout, Hudl) for professional and semi-professional tiers
- Mobile app required for coaches and players (attendance, availability, live stats entry)
- Offline capability for match-day use in venues with poor connectivity
- COPPA/GDPR considerations for youth athlete data; parental consent workflows for under-18 profiles
